CareVocal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · Applies to the CareVocal Android app (com.ambermindstudio.carebridge)

CareVocal is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, triage, or give medical advice. It records what you choose to log about how you feel and helps you share that record with your own doctor and family.

The short version

  • Your health log lives on your phone, in an encrypted database. There is no account and no server copy.
  • Most voice check-ins are transcribed on the device, with no recording saved and nothing sent anywhere.
  • The rest are recorded and sent — only once you have connectivity — to a transcription service, purely to be turned into text. That covers every "Need Help" check-in, any language your phone cannot transcribe offline, and any check-in you tell us we misheard.
  • We never collect your location.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising trackers. The only usage data that leaves your phone is what the reminder service needs — when you last checked in, how many days in a row, and whether you opened a reminder — never what you logged.
  • Nothing is ever sent to your family or emergency contacts automatically. You tap send, every time.

Who we are

CareVocal is built and published by Ambermind Studio. For any privacy question, or to ask about the data described here, contact ambermindstudio@gmail.com.

Voice check-ins: two different routes

Which route a check-in takes decides whether any audio exists at all.

  • On-device (the default). Android's offline speech recognition turns your speech into text as you talk. The audio is never written to storage and never touches the network. Only the resulting text is saved.
  • Deferred cloud transcription. Used in two situations. Either the device cannot do it — a language with no offline pack (currently Kannada), Android versions below 12, a phone with no offline speech recogniser at all, or a transcript you told us was wrong — or we choose to keep the audio, which is what happens on every red "Need Help" check-in. The recording is saved in the app's private storage and stays on the phone until you have an internet connection. It is then sent to be transcribed, and the text is attached to the entry. The recording is not deleted once the transcript arrives; it stays until you delete the check-in.

If you reject a transcript — "That's not what I said" — the wrong text is deleted, not kept alongside a correction. A sentence you have disowned should never reach your doctor.

Location

CareVocal does not request, collect, store, or transmit your location — including in the emergency alert message. The app declares no location permissions.

Emergency and family features

The red flow does three things, in this order: it records the entry, it opens your messaging app with an alert already written out to your trusted contacts, and it opens your phone dialer with the emergency number already typed in. You tap send. You tap call. CareVocal never sends a message or places a call on its own. The alert contains a fixed message and the time — no location, no transcript.

Sharing the doctor summary

When you share a summary, CareVocal renders it as an image and hands it to whichever app you pick — usually WhatsApp. That is your action and your choice of recipient. Once it leaves CareVocal, the image is governed by that app's privacy policy, not this one. A copy is written to the app's private cache to make the share possible.

Permissions we ask for

  • Microphone — to record or transcribe a voice check-in. Only while you are holding a check-in button. Green "Feeling Good" check-ins never use the microphone.
  • Notifications — for check-in reminders. CareVocal explains what the reminders are and only then asks; saying no changes nothing else in the app.

CareVocal does not request permission to send SMS or place calls. It hands the message and the number to your own apps instead, so the final action is always yours.

How long we keep things

Everything stays on your device until you delete the check-in, uninstall the app, or clear its storage; we set no expiry, because a health record is worth more the longer it runs. Recordings sent for transcription are processed and not retained by the transcription provider afterwards. If you buy the upgrade, RevenueCat keeps your purchase record for as long as needed to honour and restore it.

How to delete your data, or ask us to

CareVocal (published by Ambermind Studio) keeps your health record on your own phone, so most deletion is something you do directly and it takes effect immediately.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. One check-in: open CareVocal, find the check-in in the list on the home screen, tap Delete, and confirm. You get a few seconds to undo it. After that the check-in, its transcript, and its voice recording are erased from the phone permanently.
  2. Everything: uninstall CareVocal, or go to Android Settings → Apps → CareVocal → Storage → Clear storage. Either one erases every entry, transcript, voice recording, and your profile.

Because none of that is held on a server, these actions are complete deletions — there is no copy left anywhere for us to hold.

Ask us to delete what a third party holds

Email ambermindstudio@gmail.com from any address, with the subject "Delete my data". Tell us roughly when you installed CareVocal so we can locate the right records. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Your other choices

  • Decline the microphone permission — one-tap logging, the summary, and the red flow all still work.
  • Leave notifications off, and you will never receive a reminder. The reminder service still gets your last check-in time and streak, as described above; email us to have that deleted.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the GDPR to access, correct, or erase your personal data. Because your health data is held on your own device and not on our servers, you can exercise most of these directly in the app.

Children

CareVocal is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. An adult may log entries on behalf of a family member they care for.

Security

The local database is encrypted; recordings are held in app-private storage that other apps cannot read; all network traffic uses HTTPS; API keys live on our server, never inside the app. No system is perfect, but health data is treated as the most sensitive category here, and the architecture keeps it on the device by default.

Changes to this policy

If what CareVocal collects changes, this page is updated before the change ships, and the date at the top changes with it.
