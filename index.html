CareBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026 · Applies to the CareBridge Android app (com.ambermindstudio.carebridge)

CareBridge is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, triage, or give medical advice. It records what you choose to log about how you feel and helps you share that record with your own doctor and family.

The short version

  • Your health log lives on your phone, in an encrypted database. There is no account and no server copy.
  • Most voice check-ins are transcribed on the device, with no recording saved and nothing sent anywhere.
  • The rest are recorded and sent — only once you have connectivity — to a transcription service, purely to be turned into text. That covers every "Need Help" check-in, any language your phone cannot transcribe offline, and any check-in you tell us we misheard.
  • We never collect your location.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Nothing is ever sent to your family or emergency contacts automatically. You tap send, every time.

Who we are

CareBridge is built and published by Ambermind Studio. For any privacy question, or to ask about the data described here, contact ambermindstudio@gmail.com.

This database is encrypted at rest with SQLCipher. The encryption key is generated randomly on your device, sealed with a key held in the Android Keystore (hardware-backed where your phone supports it), and never leaves the phone. Automatic Android backup is switched off for this app, so your health data is not copied to Google Drive.

Uninstalling CareBridge deletes all of it — entries, transcripts, recordings, and profile. There is no copy anywhere else for us to hold.

Voice check-ins: two different routes

Which route a check-in takes decides whether any audio exists at all.

  • On-device (the default). Android's offline speech recognition turns your speech into text as you talk. The audio is never written to storage and never touches the network. Only the resulting text is saved.
  • Deferred cloud transcription. Used in two situations. Either the device cannot do it — a language with no offline pack (currently Kannada), Android versions below 12, or a transcript you told us was wrong — or we choose to keep the audio, which is what happens on every red "Need Help" check-in. The recording is saved in the app's private storage and stays on the phone until you have an internet connection. It is then sent to be transcribed, and the text is attached to the entry. The recording is not deleted once the transcript arrives; it stays until you delete the check-in.

If you reject a transcript — "That's not what I said" — the wrong text is deleted, not kept alongside a correction. A sentence you have disowned should never reach your doctor.

Location

CareBridge does not request, collect, store, or transmit your location — including in the emergency alert message. The app declares no location permissions.

Emergency and family features

The red flow does three things, in this order: it records the entry, it opens your messaging app with an alert already written out to your trusted contacts, and it opens your phone dialer with the emergency number already typed in. You tap send. You tap call. CareBridge never sends a message or places a call on its own. The alert contains a fixed message and the time — no location, no transcript.

Sharing the doctor summary

When you share a summary, CareBridge renders it as an image and hands it to whichever app you pick — usually WhatsApp. That is your action and your choice of recipient. Once it leaves CareBridge, the image is governed by that app's privacy policy, not this one. A copy is written to the app's private cache to make the share possible.

Permissions we ask for

  • Microphone — to record or transcribe a voice check-in. Only while you are holding a check-in button. Green "Feeling Good" check-ins never use the microphone.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on check-in reminders.

CareBridge does not request permission to send SMS or place calls. It hands the message and the number to your own apps instead, so the final action is always yours.

How long we keep things

Everything stays on your device until you delete the check-in, uninstall the app, or clear its storage; we set no expiry, because a health record is worth more the longer it runs. Recordings sent for transcription are processed and not retained by the transcription provider afterwards. If you buy the upgrade, RevenueCat keeps your purchase record for as long as needed to honour and restore it.

Your choices

  • Delete any single check-in from your history in the app. You get a few seconds to undo it; after that the check-in, its transcript, and its voice recording are erased from the phone permanently. There is no server copy to survive them.
  • Uninstall the app to erase everything CareBridge holds about you — entries, transcripts, recordings, and profile. Because none of it is on a server, uninstalling is a complete deletion.
  • Clear the app's storage from Android Settings to erase the same data without removing the app.
  • Decline the microphone permission — one-tap logging, the summary, and the red flow all still work.
  • Email us at ambermindstudio@gmail.com to ask what a third party listed above holds, or to have it deleted. We will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the GDPR to access, correct, or erase your personal data. Because your health data is held on your own device and not on our servers, you can exercise most of these directly in the app.

Children

CareBridge is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. An adult may log entries on behalf of a family member they care for.

Security

The local database is encrypted; recordings are held in app-private storage that other apps cannot read; all network traffic uses HTTPS; API keys live on our server, never inside the app. No system is perfect, but health data is treated as the most sensitive category here, and the architecture keeps it on the device by default.

Changes to this policy

If what CareBridge collects changes, this page is updated before the change ships, and the date at the top changes with it.
